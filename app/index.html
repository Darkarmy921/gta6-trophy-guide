    How this guide is built

    • Official data: every name, description and grade is quoted exactly as PlayStation publishes it (55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze).
    • Guides: written in the author's own words for this guide; each one names its sources beside it. Where a step or count is not stated in the official description, the guide says so instead of inventing it.
    • Missable flags & roadmap: derived only from what the official descriptions imply — a named story mission, a character's final mission, or a one-off choice or event. PlayStation does not confirm any trophy permanently missable, so flags mean “do it on the first pass”.
    • Online: the official list holds no online trophies, so the Online filter is empty by design.
